Tips for the best memes

  • Impact font is the classic choice — bold, all-caps, with a black outline on white backgrounds.
  • Keep captions short: 3–6 words per line land harder than full sentences.
  • Wide landscape images (16:9) work best — plenty of space for top and bottom text.
  • For dark images, switch text colour to white with a dark outline. For light images, try dark text with no outline.
  • After downloading, share directly to WhatsApp, X (Twitter), Instagram Stories, or Reddit.
  • Make multiple versions — try different font sizes or colour combos and download each one.
