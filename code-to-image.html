💡 Tips for stunning code images

  • Use 2× or 3× resolution for sharp images on Retina/HiDPI screens.
  • Keep snippets short and focused — 5–20 lines look best on social media.
  • Dracula and One Dark themes look great on dark feeds; GitHub Light pops on white backgrounds.
  • Match your background gradient to your brand or the mood of the post.
  • Turn off line numbers for very short snippets to keep it clean.
  • Add your website watermark to drive traffic back to your profile.
