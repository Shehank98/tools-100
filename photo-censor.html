💡 How to use Photo Censor

  • Draw — drag to draw a rectangle over the area you want to hide.
  • Pixelate — classic mosaic effect; drag the Intensity slider to control block size.
  • Blur — smooth Gaussian-style blur; great for faces.
  • Black / White bar — solid opaque bars, the traditional censoring method.
  • Emoji — place a repeating emoji (e.g. 😊) over the region for a fun cover-up.
  • Custom color — fill with any colour, with adjustable opacity.
  • Add multiple regions with different effects before downloading.
