🙋 Who We Are

ToolBox is a free online platform built by a small team of developers and designers who got tired of hunting across dozens of different websites just to do simple everyday tasks - counting words in a document, converting units, or generating a QR code. We believed these tools should live in one place, work instantly, and be available to everyone at no cost.

We are independent developers passionate about building useful, accessible software. We are not backed by a big corporation. We are just a dedicated team who believe that the best tools on the internet should be free, fast, and completely private.

💡 Why We Built ToolBox

Most "free" tool websites come with frustrating trade-offs - annoying sign-up walls, file size limits, mandatory email subscriptions, slow servers, or hidden paywalls. We experienced all of these firsthand and decided to build something better.

ToolBox was created with three guiding principles:

⚡ Speed First

Every tool on ToolBox runs entirely inside your browser. There is no server upload, no waiting in a queue. You get results in milliseconds.

🔒 Privacy by Design

We never see your files, text, or data. Images, PDFs, and passwords are all processed locally on your device and never transmitted to any server.

🆓 Forever Free

Every tool on this website is, and always will be, completely free to use. We sustain the platform through non-intrusive advertising, not paywalls.
