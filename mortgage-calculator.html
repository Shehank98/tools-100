💡 Tips

  • A 15-year mortgage saves tens of thousands in interest vs 30-year, but monthly payments are higher.
  • Even a 0.5% rate reduction on a $300k loan saves over $30,000 over 30 years.
  • Making one extra payment per year towards principal can cut years off your loan.
  • Property tax and insurance are typically bundled into your escrow / monthly payment.
