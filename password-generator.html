🛡️ Password Security Tips

  • Use at least 12 characters for any important account.
  • Use a different password for every account — never reuse.
  • Store passwords in a trusted password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password).
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever available.
  • Avoid passwords based on your name, birthday or common dictionary words.
  • Change your passwords after any known data breach.
