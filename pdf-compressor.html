💡 PDF Compression Tips

  • PDFs with many images compress the most — up to 80% reduction.
  • Text-only PDFs will see minimal compression since text is already compact.
  • Use Light mode for important documents where quality matters (legal, medical).
  • Use Heavy mode for drafts, references, or when email attachment size is the issue.
  • All processing is done entirely in your browser — your PDF stays private.
