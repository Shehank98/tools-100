💡 QR Code Tips

  • Always test your QR code with your phone before printing.
  • Use a larger size (300px+) for printed materials like posters or flyers.
  • Ensure high contrast between QR color and background for reliable scanning.
  • Short URLs scan faster and more reliably than long ones — use a URL shortener if needed.
  • Wi-Fi QR codes let guests connect without typing the password.
